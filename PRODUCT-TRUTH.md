> Reference for all ad copy. Do not claim features not listed here.

## The Market Context

**Target:** Trade show / expo exhibitors in India.
**Core promise:** Walk into an expo with just your phone or laptop — no printed materials — and walk out with more leads than your competitors.

---

## The Villain — How Exhibitors Collect Leads Today (and Why It Breaks)

Each method below is a valid ad angle. 10 EDU ad variants should each attack one.

### 1. Pen and Paper
- Illegible handwriting → data lost before you even enter it
- Physical papers get lost at a busy show
- Takes hours to digitize after the event
- Crowds the booth — visitors queue to write, others wait outside
- Looks unprofessional / outdated to visitors

### 2. Excel Sheet on Laptop
- One device = one bottleneck — queue forms instantly
- Confusing columns — visitors don't know what to fill where
- All visitors' data visible to each other — privacy problem
- One accidental delete or laptop crash → all leads gone

### 3. Google Forms
- Doesn't load on busy expo floors — network congestion is the norm
- Completely useless with no connectivity
- Visitors bounce before the form loads

### 4. Expo/Organizer App
- Requires the visitor to have the app downloaded — less than 40% do
- Even if downloaded, visitors delete it within a week of the event
- Exhibitors can't rely on visitors going home to fill something
- Offline mode usually broken or nonexistent

### 5. Printing QR → Social Media (Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp / YouTube)
- Doesn't work offline or on slow expo networks
- Visitor follows you — but you're lost in the noise of their existing feed
- No lead data captured — just a vanity follow with no follow-up path

### 6. Printing QR → Website
- Doesn't load without internet or loads slowly on congested networks
- Ends up in one of 100 open browser tabs — closed, never revisited
- No lead captured — just an anonymous visit

### 7. Printing QR → PDF Catalog (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.)
- Files are 10MB+ — download starts, visitor walks away before it opens
- Doesn't work offline at all
- No lead captured — just a failed file download

### 8. Business Cards + Printed Brochures
- High printing costs — spent before a single lead is captured
- End up in the bin within a week of the event
- QR codes on them lead to social/website (same problems as #5 and #6 above)
- You run out mid-show and miss leads in the last sessions

### 9. Rented Physical Badge Scanner
- No real-time view of captured leads
- No context can be added at point of scan — just raw contact data
- Expensive rental with no ownership of the data experience
- Data handed to you as a bulk file after the show — cold by then

## CallCards — What It Actually Does (Confirmed Features Only)

### One QR Code = Everything
A single QR covers:
- Your digital business card
- Your product catalog / brochure
- Your lead capture form

No printing required. No multiple links. One scan, everything accessible.

### Booth Mode
- Displays QR + lead form on one screen
- **Works offline** — syncs all captured leads when connectivity returns
- Open on multiple devices simultaneously:
  - Staff log in to the same account, OR
  - Scan the "Share Booth Mode" QR from the main device — generates a tokenized link that opens booth mode on any device
- All leads from all devices save to one dashboard, one account
- No equipment purchase or rental — works on any existing phone or laptop

### Visitor Flow (First Scan)
1. Visitor scans exhibitor's QR
2. Sees exhibitor's digital card
3. Card is saved to visitor's CallCards wallet / card organizer
4. Visitor is shown a lead form
5. Visitor fills form → their own free digital card is created
6. From the second scan onwards: visitor just taps to share their card back — no form fill again

> **Key angle:** The visitor gets a free digital business card for life. They come back to it.

### Badge Scanner
- Scans any QR code at the event
- If the QR is a CallCards or vCard → saved under the **Cards** tab
- If the QR is raw content (URL, text, plain data) → saved under the **Scans** tab
- Notes and Favorites available on both types — add context the moment you scan
- **Advantage over rented physical scanners:** real-time view, notes, favorites, no bulk-file delivery

### Lead Context
- Add a note immediately after any scan: what they were interested in, timeline, decision-maker status
- Mark as Favorite to prioritize hot leads
- Context travels with the lead — no reconstruction from memory days later

### Dashboard + Export
- All leads from all devices sync to one dashboard
- Easy export for faster follow-up
- Follow up while leads are still hot → higher conversion
- Works with any CRM or outreach tool the exhibitor already uses

## What CallCards Does NOT Do
> Do not claim these in any ad.

- ❌ Does **not** send automatic follow-up messages
- ❌ Does **not** auto-fill the lead form from a badge scan (visitor fills it)
- ❌ Does **not** guarantee offline catalog viewing (catalog needs connectivity; card ID saved for resync)
- ❌ The product does not "follow up for you" — it gives you the organized data to follow up yourself, fast

## Tone & Voice
- Confident, direct — not salesy
- Speak to the pain of the broken method first, then show the fix
- Indian trade show context — lakhs spent, booth culture, expo floors
- Avoid: "follow-up sends itself", "automatically", any claim of automation that doesn't exist
