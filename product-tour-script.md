# call.cards — 90-Second Product Tour Script + Storyboard
> Target audience: Exhibitors (Priya the booth operator + cost-conscious founder)
> Format: Screen-recorded product demo with VO, text overlays, real device footage

---

## Music / Tone Direction
Dry, understated percussion — clean hi-hats, minimal bass. Think product launch reel, not hype reel. No swells. No drop. Tempo matches narration, not the other way around.

---

## Script

---

### Scene 1 | 0:00 – 0:08

**Visual:** Close-up B-roll. A thick stack of paper lead forms on a table. A hand drops a business card on top. Cut to a tired exhibitor typing names from paper into a laptop at the end of the day.

**Narration:**
"Every expo ends the same way. Paper forms. Business cards. A spreadsheet you fill in at midnight — hoping you can read the handwriting."

**Text overlay:** *(none)*

**Notes for editor:** Cold open. No logo. No music intro. Cut on the word "handwriting." Hard cut to Scene 2 — no crossfade.

---

### Scene 2 | 0:08 – 0:16

**Visual:** Phone screen. Browser opens to call.cards booth form. 6 fields visible: Name, Phone, Email, Designation, Company, Notes. Visitor hands are shown typing into the form. No app store. No download prompt.

**Narration:**
"call.cards is a booth form. It lives in the browser. Visitors type directly on your device. No app to download. Nothing to install."

**Text overlay:** `call.cards` — bottom left, small

**Notes for editor:** Screen recording, real device. Zoom in on the URL bar to show it's just a browser. 1.5x playback speed on the typing.

---

### Scene 3 | 0:16 – 0:25

**Visual:** Split screen. Left: phone on booth table. Right: tablet held by another team member. Both show the same booth dashboard with the same leads populating in real time. 3 new leads appear while we watch.

**Narration:**
"Every device on your team logs into the same booth account. One person captures. Everyone sees it. Laptop, phone, iPad — it doesn't matter."

**Text overlay:** `All team devices. One lead list.`

**Notes for editor:** Animate the lead count ticking up (+1, +2, +3) in sync with narration beat. Real-time sync effect — highlight the matching entries on both screens briefly.

---

### Scene 4 | 0:25 – 0:31

**Visual:** WiFi icon on phone goes grey — "No Connection." Visitor types into the booth form anyway. Form submits. "Saved offline" indicator appears. Then WiFi icon returns — lead syncs silently.

**Narration:**
"WiFi dies at every expo. The form doesn't care. It saves offline and syncs the moment you're back on the network."

**Text overlay:** `Offline-first. Always.`

**Notes for editor:** Icon animation should feel matter-of-fact, not dramatic. Short scene — keep it punchy.

---

### Scene 5 | 0:31 – 0:42

**Visual:** Exhibitor holds phone, opens badge scanner. Camera frame appears. Attendee holds up their badge QR. Scanner reads it. "Lead saved — note added" screen appears. Whole interaction shown in real time: 3 seconds.

**Narration:**
"Badge scanner. Point your phone at an attendee's badge QR. Add a note. Done. 2 to 3 seconds per lead. When the organiser releases contact data after the expo, it populates automatically."

**Text overlay:** `2–3 seconds per lead`

**Notes for editor:** This scene should feel fast. Show the real-time clock or a subtle timer on-screen if possible. Cut immediately after "Done." — don't linger.

---

### Scene 6 | 0:42 – 0:50

**Visual:** Busy booth moment. Multiple visitors. Exhibitor scans 3 badges in quick succession. Cut to dashboard — lead count jumps: 12 → 13 → 14 → 15. Each entry shows timestamp and note.

**Narration:**
"In a rush hour at the booth, you're not stopping to type anything. Scan, note, next person. The queue moves."

**Text overlay:** *(none — let the visual speak)*

**Notes for editor:** High energy, but no music swell. Fast cuts between scanner and dashboard. Real footage preferred.

---

### Scene 7 | 0:50 – 1:02

**Visual:** Laptop screen. Booth dashboard. Left column: lead names and timestamps. Right panel: individual lead detail — all 6 fields populated, note visible. Scroll to show the full list. 20+ leads visible.

**Narration:**
"Every capture lands here. Name, phone, email, company, designation, your note. All searchable. All in one place. No one transcribes anything."

**Text overlay:** `All leads. One dashboard.`

**Notes for editor:** Clean screen recording. Cursor moves naturally — not scripted-looking. Scroll speed should feel human. Timestamp column visible throughout.

---

### Scene 8 | 1:02 – 1:10

**Visual:** Dashboard. Cursor clicks "Export CSV." A small modal: "Export Pass — ₹1,000 per day of expo." Cursor clicks confirm. CSV downloads. File opens in Excel — rows of clean lead data visible.

**Narration:**
"Capture is free. Always. When the show is done and you want the spreadsheet — ₹1,000 per day of expo. That's it. No subscription. No per-lead charge."

**Text overlay:** `Free to capture. ₹1,000 to export.`

**Notes for editor:** Emphasise "Always" with a slight pause before it. The CSV open in Excel is important — shows the output is familiar, not locked in a proprietary format.

---

### Scene 9 | 1:10 – 1:20

**Visual:** Screen pulls back to show a full expo hall. Overlay animation: call.cards logo centred. Three icons appear around it — booth form icon, badge scanner icon, dashboard icon. Simple. Clean.

**Narration:**
"Booth form. Badge scanner. Live dashboard. All your leads, ready the same day."

**Text overlay:** `call.cards` — large, centred
Below: `Free to capture. ₹1,000 to export.`

**Notes for editor:** This is the only moment with logo animation. Keep it under 1.5 seconds. No particle effects. No glow. Clean fade in.

---

### Scene 10 | 1:20 – 1:30

**Visual:** Single CTA screen. Clean white or dark background. URL centred: **call.cards**. No QR code clutter — just the URL and one line below it.

**Narration:**
"Set up in under 5 minutes. No hardware. No contract. Start free at call.cards."

**Text overlay:**
`call.cards`
`Start free. No credit card.`

**Notes for editor:** Hold this frame for 4 full seconds after VO ends. Let it breathe. Music fades out cleanly on the last word of narration.

At 130 words/minute narration pace: **~117 seconds** (1 min 57 sec) of pure VO.

**Action required:** The scenes run slightly long at a natural narration pace. Two options:

1. **Trim narration** — Scenes 5 and 8 have the most room. Scene 5 can cut "When the organiser releases contact data after the expo, it populates automatically." to a text overlay instead of VO, saving ~7 seconds.
2. **Increase pace** — Narrate Scenes 1–4 at ~140 wpm (punchy, dry) and slow to 120 wpm for Scenes 8–10 (pricing, CTA). This lands at ~105 seconds, comfortably within 90 seconds of total runtime when accounting for silent visual-only moments in Scenes 6 and 9.

**Recommended cut:** Remove the sentence beginning "When the organiser releases..." from Scene 5 VO. Move it to a text overlay: `Contact details auto-populate after the expo.` This brings total VO to ~230 words / ~106 seconds, fitting a 90-second cut with room for musical tail.

## 3 Alternative Opening Hooks (A/B Test)

**Hook A — The number:**
> "The average exhibitor loses 40% of leads between expo day and the first follow-up. Not because the leads weren't good. Because the data was on paper."

*Use for:* Persona C (VP, pipeline-focused). Leads with a hard stat before showing the product.

---

**Hook B — The moment:**
> "It's 3pm on Day 2. Your booth is packed. Someone hands you a business card and walks away. You will never find that person again."

*Use for:* Persona A (Priya, booth operator). Visceral. Specific. No numbers needed — the situation is the argument.

---

**Hook C — The cost:**
> "You spent ₹25,000 on printed brochures for this show. Most of them are in a bin outside Hall 4. This is a different way."

*Use for:* Persona B (cost-conscious founder). Attacks the budget before offering the alternative. High contrast.
